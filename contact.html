What is your shipping policy?

We offer standard shipping (5-7 business days) and expedited shipping (2-3 business days) options. Orders over $35 qualify for free standard shipping within the United States. International shipping rates vary by location.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Books must be in their original condition and not damaged. Please get in touch with our customer service team to initiate a return.

About Cold Case Publishing

Cold Case Publishing was founded in 2020 by passionate crime fiction enthusiasts who saw a need for a publishing house dedicated exclusively to high-quality crime thrillers. What began as a small independent press has quickly grown into a respected name in the industry.

Our mission is simple: to bring readers the most compelling crime fiction from established authors and emerging voices. We believe in the power of a well-crafted mystery to captivate, challenge, and entertain readers.

Echoes in the Ozarks

by Eric Wallace

Book 1 of 4: The Ozarks Series

$24.99

Deep in the misty mountains of the Ozarks, a decades-old mystery resurfaces when Detective Morgan Hayes returns to her hometown to investigate a series of disappearances that mirror those from her childhood. As she delves deeper into the case, she uncovers dark secrets that some in the small community would kill to keep buried.

With atmospheric prose and nail-biting suspense, "Echoes in the Ozarks" is the first installment in Eric Wallace's gripping four-book series that explores the haunting power of the past and the lengths people will go to protect their secrets.

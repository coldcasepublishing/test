About Cold Case Publishing

Dedicated to the art of suspense and mystery

Our Story

Cold Case Publishing was founded in 2020 by passionate crime fiction enthusiasts who saw a need for a publishing house dedicated exclusively to high-quality crime thrillers. What began as a small independent press has quickly grown into a respected name in the industry, known for discovering exceptional new talent and publishing gripping, page-turning stories.

Our mission is simple: to bring readers the most compelling crime fiction from established authors and emerging voices. We believe in the power of a well-crafted mystery to captivate, challenge, and entertain readers, and we're committed to publishing books that do just that.

Our Philosophy

At Cold Case Publishing, we believe that great crime fiction does more than entertain—it explores the complexities of human nature, challenges our perceptions, and keeps us turning pages late into the night. We're dedicated to finding stories that do all these things.

We take pride in our meticulous editorial process, working closely with our authors to ensure each book reaches its full potential. From the initial manuscript to the final published work, we're committed to excellence at every stage.

We also believe in building a community around our books. That's why we host regular events, book clubs, and author Q&As, creating spaces where readers can connect with the writers they love.

Our Team

Cold Case Publishing is made up of a small but dedicated team of professionals who are passionate about crime fiction. Everyone at Cold Case is committed to bringing the best possible books to our readers, from our editors and designers to our marketing and sales staff.
